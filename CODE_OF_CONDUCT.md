# Code of conduct

Notaboek's issues and discussions are for the people who use it. Keep them kind, useful and on topic.

## Expected

- Be respectful, including when you disagree. Argue with ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming otherwise.
- Keep feedback specific: what happened, where, and what would be better.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, personal attacks, insults or threats.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Sharing someone's personal details or private files without their permission.
- Spam, advertising or repeated off-topic posts.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, lock or close threads, and block accounts that break this code. Most problems get a warning first. Serious or repeated ones don't.

## Reporting

Use **Report content** in the **…** menu on the comment, issue or discussion. You can send the report to the maintainer, to GitHub, or both. Reports are kept private.

This code applies in this repository's issues, discussions and anywhere else someone speaks for the project.
